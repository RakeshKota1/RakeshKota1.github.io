RK-DECK

Educational content automation for exam-prep current affairs

About

RK-DECK is a personal, locally-run automation tool that generates and publishes educational video content for Indian government competitive exam preparation (UPSC/SSC/banking-style current affairs, and daily programming concept lessons). It runs entirely on its developer's own computer and publishes finished videos to its own YouTube channels.

What this app does

RK-DECK analyzes publicly available news articles, generates exam-focused educational scripts and video content, and uploads the finished videos directly to its operator's own YouTube channels. It also reads basic YouTube Analytics data (views, watch time) for those same channels to inform publishing decisions.

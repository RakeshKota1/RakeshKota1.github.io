Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

RK-DECK ("the app") is a personal, locally-run content automation tool operated by a single individual developer for their own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses via Google APIs and how that data is used.

What we access

RK-DECK requests the following Google OAuth scopes, solely for the developer's own YouTube channels (not on behalf of any other user):

  • youtube.upload — to upload video files the app generates to the developer's own YouTube channel(s).
  • youtube.force-ssl — to manage metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists) on videos the app uploads.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read basic performance metrics (views, watch time) on the developer's own channel(s), used only to inform when future content is published.

How this data is used

All data accessed through these scopes is used exclusively to operate the developer's own YouTube channels: publishing videos the app generates, and reading that same channel's own performance data. No data is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

Where data is stored

All application data — including OAuth tokens, generated content, and analytics data — is stored locally on the developer's own computer in a local database. Nothing is transmitted to or stored on any third-party server beyond Google's own APIs (used to upload content and read the developer's own channel analytics) and the AI providers used to generate content.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained locally for as long as the developer continues operating the application and can be deleted at any time by removing the local database. OAuth access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data usage changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to rkdeskofficial@gmail.com.
